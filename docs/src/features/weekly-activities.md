# Weekly activities and Daily Progress

The web **Exercises → Workout Plans** view and the phone **Daily Progress** screen
show the selected calendar week. Running, walking, cycling, strength training,
soccer, mobility and stretching use the existing Workout Plans and Mobility
planners. No automatic training program is generated.

## Plan and record

1. Create a weekly Workout Plan with weekday assignments. Presets can contain
   strength sets or timed/distance activities. Mobility schedules create dated
   plans through the existing planner. Sequential workouts retain their next-session
   workflow and do not create dated weekly tasks.
2. Log the workout in Diary or complete the planned routine in Mobility.
3. Review **Weekly activities**. A workout becomes complete when confirmed sets
   in one saved session cover every exercise in its saved prescription. Pending
   prefills, duplicate sessions and elapsed timers alone do not imply completion.
   Mobility requires completed outcomes for every routine step.
4. For an imported or manually logged session, choose an unassigned, confirmed
   Diary session on the same date and select **Link activity**. This records your
   explicit decision. A session cannot satisfy two scheduled activities.
5. Select **Skip activity** to remove that task from the Daily Progress denominator,
   or **Undo decision** to remove your skip/link. These controls do not change Diary
   data, calories, provider provenance or set completion.

Weekly counts describe scheduled tasks rather than all exercise volume. Each
applicable daily task counts equally in Daily Progress; the X is not a health score.
Partly confirmed routines remain started. Removing the linked record removes its
completion evidence. If another device changes a decision, refresh and try again.

## History and privacy

Saved plan versions preserve earlier prescriptions. Existing plans receive a
baseline from the migration day; prescriptions for earlier days are unknown and
are omitted from quantified completion coverage. The calendar keeps those days
unknown. Recorded or explicitly resolved activities retain their original dated
prescription when a plan is changed or deleted later on the same day.
Library/preset edits do not rewrite the saved version: save the Workout Plan again
to capture its updated prescription. A name-based activity category is an inference.
Unsynced phone/Watch activity is absent from server reads.

The overview and decisions are available only for your own account. They are not
shared through family diary/report permissions. Agents can read the plans and
confirmation states through the dedicated [MCP tools](./mcp-server), while the
owner makes linking/skipping decisions in the application.
