# Sharing

X on Track may offer various ways to share your fitness data, including exporting data or sharing with other applications. Details on these sharing functionalities will be added in the future.

## Weekly plans and recording progress

Weekly plans may contain training types, saved routines and several sessions per day. Existing workout-library and diary sharing permissions are unchanged. Daily Progress and activity-definition preparation are owner-only. A plan is separate from a completed workout; editing or deleting it does not erase recorded diary snapshots. Import-only meal groups remain available with their historical entries but are excluded from routine progress tasks.
