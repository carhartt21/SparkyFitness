# Check-In

The Check-In feature in X on Track allows you to quickly log your daily progress, including meals, exercises, and other key metrics. Detailed instructions on using the Check-In feature will be provided in the future.

## Daily Check-In

The Daily Check-In (mobile: **More → Daily Check-In** or the Dashboard's Daily Progress card) takes a few taps:

- **How was your day overall?** Very difficult, difficult, okay, good or great.
- **Day summary** (optional, 1–5): energy, stress, sleep last night, nutrition on track and activity. Each scale states what 1 and 5 mean. For stress, a higher number means more stress, so it is never shown as better.
- **Note and tags** such as busy day or low sleep, plus your own tags.

**Complete** needs at least one answer, note or tag. **Skip for today** records that you chose not to check in; it stores no answers and you can reopen the day. Leaving with unsaved answers keeps them as a draft.

## Health context

Under **More → Health context** you can note an injury (with body area and limitation), an illness or a vacation, backdated and overlapping if needed. A period never changes your records or schedules. You can choose to pause optional reminders (hydration, meal photos, movement, check-in, habits and weigh-ins) while it is active; medication and supplement reminders always continue.

## Daily Progress

Daily Progress is the share of today's explicit tracking tasks you completed: the check-in, scheduled habits, scheduled supplements, weigh-ins you chose to count, and, if enabled, meals you mark complete or "no meal". Each task counts equally. A task you explicitly skip leaves the count. Logged foods alone never complete a meal. When nothing applies, the X stays neutral. Choose what counts under **More → Tracking settings**.
