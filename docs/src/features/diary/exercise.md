# Exercise Tracking

X on Track lets you record activities and workouts, including duration, sets, reps, weight and available calories. Health synchronization uses the [Mobile App](/mobile-app/mobile-app); a synced workout record is not a reusable workout preset.

Use [Weekly training plans](/features/exercises/weekly-planning) to schedule whole activities or saved presets. Saving a plan does not record completed training. Start a preset in the phone app for live set tracking and Watch synchronization, or log an activity with the actual duration/distance after performing it.

[MCP-based training](/features/exercises/mcp-training) can help inspect history and maintain presets. MCP diary logging does not start a live session or guarantee an Apple Health workout export.

For high-intensity conditioning, interval timers, and CrossFit-style WOD scoring, check out the dedicated [Interval, Circuit & WOD Workouts](/features/exercises/interval-wod-workouts) guide.

## Daily Training on the phone

Open the full-width **Training** card on Home to inspect the selected day. Recorded
workouts appear before plans, with their source times, actual duration and available
distance/energy. A daily active-calorie aggregate is not listed as a workout.
Canonical imported activity names are translated; custom workout names stay as
entered. Unknown energy is not replaced with a fabricated calorie estimate.

The summary includes recorded workouts and terminal mobility sessions. Mobility
minutes describe elapsed session time, including pauses, rather than measured
active exercise time. **Start Workout**, **Log training** and **Mobility** reuse
existing recording flows. **Weekly training plan** opens the dated itinerary;
**Review & history** retains the existing exercise review and import-range tools.
Opening either view never marks a plan complete or exports a workout.
