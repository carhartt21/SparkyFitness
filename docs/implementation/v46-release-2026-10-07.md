`feat/v45-diary-meals-training-refinement-20261007` and
`feat/deferred-task-completion-20261007` without conflicts. The source retains the

weekly activity goals were already integrated. The retained micronutrient backup
is excluded and its rollout gates remain disabled. Older dirty checkouts and
private deployment edits remain preserved: they contain stale, superseded or
unfinished work, rather than additional approved release features.
