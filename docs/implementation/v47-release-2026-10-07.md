follow-ups without conflicts:

- [Intake gauge optical spacing](intake-card-progress-2026-10-07.md): moves the
  four-line value stack slightly lower inside the existing gauge at ordinary
  text sizes, retaining the enlarged-text layout and unchanged card dimensions.

  adds a direct live-start action and carries the saved assignment through routine

  distance target. Separate sessions are not combined into a completed goal;
  the phone explains the recorded metrics and missing or ambiguous evidence.

main, and the older weekly-activity branch was already incorporated with its
classification/date fixes. The micronutrient backup remains excluded and its
