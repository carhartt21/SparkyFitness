# Stage 3 progress photos

The progress-photo screen now opens an in-app camera for front, back, and side captures. A fixed pose outline is shown by default and can be hidden before taking a picture. The outline is rendered over the live preview; the capture path passes only `CameraView.takePictureAsync`'s photo to the image preparer and upload API, so it is not embedded in stored image bytes. Library imports follow the existing picker path and do not show the live guide.

The current mobile upload policy converts captures and library selections to JPEG at quality 0.85 and caps the long edge at 1600 px. After a camera upload attempt, the app best-effort deletes the temporary camera original and prepared JPEG from its cache. The server stores the uploaded JPEG as the single photo for the user, day, and angle; it does not retain a separate original or generate a derivative. The original remains in the device camera/library only if the platform saved it there independently; the app makes no such guarantee. Synthetic unit tests cover resize and encoding arguments, guide separation, and cache cleanup. They do not establish image quality, backup, or restore behavior.

Before treating the photo gate as complete, compare representative synthetic portrait and side photos on a device at the current compression setting, verify camera permission and cancellation, background/foreground behavior, protected preview access, replacement/deletion, and restore from a server backup. Choose whether retaining originals is required after that quality and restore review. These physical and operations checks remain pending in the separate device checklist.
