# Food fallback artwork

The saved or provider image remains the first choice. When it is absent or fails to load, mobile and web choose a **display-only** illustration. Open Food Facts search and barcode results now use its `food_groups_tags`, derived from its category taxonomy. Other providers and saved foods fall back to a conservative name classifier. The choice is never written to a food, diary entry, nutrition snapshot, or photo gallery. A saved meal always uses the meal illustration. Unrecognized names use a neutral covered dish, so an unfamiliar branded product is not depicted as a specific ingredient.

The shared classifier and complete 91-group Open Food Facts mapping live in `shared/src/foodImages/fallbackGroup.ts`. The food-group taxonomy has three levels: 13 top-level substantive groups, 42 second-level groups and 33 third-level groups, plus three unknown-group placeholders. Specific tags override broad ancestor tags. Open Food Facts' much larger category taxonomy has thousands of nodes and does not require one image per product subtype: products with no recognized group still receive an illustration via their name or the neutral generic fallback. The name classifier handles German accents, English and German names, and common compounds. Specific forms such as sauce, soup, pasta, drink, or prepared dish take precedence over ingredient words. This is visual categorization, not a nutrition or search classification.

Thirty-one illustrations cover vegetables, fruit, bread, grains, dairy, eggs, meat, seafood, legumes, nuts, drinks, sweets, condiments, meals, potatoes, breakfast cereals, oils, salty snacks, soups, cheese, ice cream, pastries, sandwiches, hot drinks, plant drinks, pasta, processed meat, baby food, alcohol, honey, and unknown food. The [updated contact sheet](evidence/food-fallback-contact-v2.png) shows the full set. All were generated with the built-in image generation tool, reviewed visually, and exported as transparent 256 px PNGs for Expo and 256 px WebP files for Vite. Mobile assets are in `XoTMobile/assets/food-fallbacks/`; web assets are in `XoTFrontend/public/images/food-fallbacks/`. The source generation output is held outside the repository; the optimized app assets are the deliverables.

For Open Food Facts results, the 31-image set is supplemented by **one asset per substantive food group**: 88 distinct illustrations for its 88 named food groups. The three “unknown food group” tags intentionally use the name-based or neutral fallback rather than implying a food identity. See the [91-group contact sheet](evidence/off-food-groups-contact.png). Group-specific files are in `XoTMobile/assets/off-food-groups/` and `XoTFrontend/public/images/off-food-groups/`. Twenty-eight groups use the corresponding reviewed food illustration; 60 groups have newly generated subjects. The static Metro map in `XoTMobile/src/utils/offFoodGroupImages.ts` lists all 88 assets. The web uses the matching group slug as its asset URL. This keeps product photographs first while making category distinctions visible when the product lacks one.

The common generation prompt specified a compact centered generic food-group thumbnail, polished natural food photography, soft studio light, transparent background, readable at 48 px, and no packaging, text, logos, people, or unrelated foods. The original subjects were tomato/carrot/greens; apple/berries; bread; rice; milk/cheese; eggs; chicken/beef; fish/shrimp; beans/chickpeas; nuts/seeds; water/juice; chocolate/cookie; red sauce; mixed meal bowl; and a covered serving dish for unknown foods. The second batch added potatoes, breakfast cereal, olive oil, savory snacks, soup, cheese, ice cream, pastries, a sandwich, coffee/tea, a plant drink, pasta, processed meat, baby food, beer/wine, and honey. Dishes and drinks use their necessary bowls or vessels. No image depicts a named provider product or promises its exact ingredients.

Search rows, food/meal libraries, and diary rows show the artwork when needed. Actual photos remain available in the image viewer; fallback art is not clickable as a product photo. The app remains usable offline because the fallback assets are bundled. A broken remote image also resolves to the appropriate bundled illustration. `food_group_tags` is a transient API/display field, not a persisted food attribute. The [Open Food Facts food-group taxonomy](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/main/taxonomies/food_groups.txt) determines the mapping; maintain it if upstream adds or changes groups.

Validation for the expanded set: all 91 food-group tags in the published taxonomy snapshot have a mapping; every substantive group has a distinct asset file, 256 px transparent PNG for Expo and WebP for Vite. The group-specific contact sheet was reviewed at thumbnail scale. Targeted web and mobile tests, type-checks, lint, web build, and iOS export are run with this change. The export is not a signed native build. Physical-device appearance still needs review in a subsequent build. Full mobile validation remains blocked by the pre-existing locale audit and an unrelated unused `GAP_USER_LABEL` export; both also occur outside these changes.
