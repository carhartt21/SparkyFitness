## iPhone visual and behavior pass

- [ ] Launcher, Spotlight, app switcher, notification, and tinted icon show the approved X geometry at small size in light and dark appearance.
- [ ] Splash, onboarding, About, settings, permission prompts, notification text, and Live Activity say **X on Track** where the product is named. **Keep getting better.** is used only as brand language, not as a false progress status.

- [ ] Home, Diary, nutrition quick actions, active workout, rest timer, widgets, and Live Activity remain usable with Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, reduced motion, and dark mode.
- [ ] On Add Food, check the photo, nutrition summary, amount/unit controls, quick portions, and meal/date/time selection in light and dark mode. With a long entry note and the keyboard open, the focused text stays visible and Add Food remains reachable; after saving, the note persists on the entry.
- [ ] Widget installation/registration, tap deep links, and lock-screen or reduced-luminance presentations use the right brand and reveal no sensitive detail by default.
