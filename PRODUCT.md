# X on Track

**Keep getting better.**

X on Track is a private health and performance companion. The X represents the dimension a person chooses to track: nutrition, training, sleep, recovery, activity, habits, health, or progress. The product loop is **Track → Understand → Adjust → Stay on track → Keep getting better**.

Progress is relative to the person's own goals and baseline. The interface is calm, directional, optimistic, and direct. It does not rank people, moralize food, invent scores, or imply diagnosis. A missed log is missing information, not a failure. An “on track” status requires supporting data; it is not decorative copy.

The approved light and dark X/track artwork is the visual reference for app icons, launch, onboarding, and About. Functional controls use semantic platform icons.
